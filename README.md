# pa-lidar-ruins

Looking for unrecorded archaeological earthworks in Pennsylvania with free USGS 3DEP lidar,
following the loop in Nate's ["I went looking for lost cities with Claude"](https://bakerbench.substack.com/)
(detector → calibrate on known sites → wide search → skeptic review → check the record → report privately).

**Status: phase 1 (detectors + calibration) in progress.** Full write-up to follow.

## Site-location policy

Exact locations of candidate (possibly unrecorded) sites are **never** committed here. Search outputs,
candidate coordinates and chips live in the git-ignored `results/private/` and are reported privately to
the Pennsylvania State Historic Preservation Office (PA SHPO / PHMC) and the land manager. Only
already-public known sites are used for calibration, and only county-level summaries are published.
